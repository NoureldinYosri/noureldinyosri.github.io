I studied computer engineering in Egypt, because of my performance in [Google Code Jam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Code_Jam) I was invited to interview for a software engineering role which led me to join google as a SWE in 2020 in an infrastructure team.

I came to quantum computing through an unsual way. I was first introduced to the field through UC Berkeley's [free online introductory course in 2014](https://www.mooc-list.com/course/quantum-mechanics-and-quantum-computation-edx), after joining google I started to seriously study quantum mechanics and quantum computing in my free time by reading books and studying online video lecutres. At the same time I started contributing to Google's quantum computing libraries [Cirq](https://github.com/quantumlib/Cirq) and [Qualtran](https://github.com/quantumlib/Qualtran) as a [20% project](https://googleblog.blogspot.com/2006/05/googles-20-percent-time-in-action.html) and after ~18 months of doing this 20% project I received an offer to move internally to the [Quantum AI team](https://quantumai.google/).

---
