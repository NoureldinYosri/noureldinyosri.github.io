). During that time, I competed in various regional and international contests, focusing mainly on Google Code Jam (GCJ) and ICPC. Later in my career I moved to creating problems and judging. 

### Achievements
- **ACM-ICPC World Finalist (2020)**: Represented AAST with team *EnthusiasmOverflow*.
- **ACM-ICPC World Finalist (2017)**: Represented Benha University (Faculty of Engineering at Shoubra) with team *The Unlooked-for*.
- **ICPC Regional Contests**: Consistently ranked in top positions across Egyptian and Arab Collegiate Programming Contests (2013–2016).

### Community Contributions
- **Problem Developer & Judge (2017–2023)**: Served as a Problem Setter, Tester, and Chief Judge for numerous ICPC regionals, including the Africa & Arab, Egyptian, Saudi, Syrian, and Lebanese Collegiate Programming Contests.
